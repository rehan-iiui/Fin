About FinanceFlow

Your personal finance dashboard.

FinanceFlow

FinanceFlow is a browser-based personal finance dashboard designed to help you record transactions, monitor budgets, manage bills, track savings, organize recurring transactions, and understand your financial activity.

Your information is stored locally in your browser. FinanceFlow does not require an external financial account for its basic features.
