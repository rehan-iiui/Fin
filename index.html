ℹ️ About FinanceFlow

FinanceFlow is a standalone personal finance dashboard designed to organize income, expenses, budgets, savings goals and bills. Your information is stored locally in your browser using localStorage.

🔒 Your data stays in this browser unless you export it.
📤 Use Export CSV to create a copy of your transaction data.
💡 This app is for organization and tracking, not financial advice.
